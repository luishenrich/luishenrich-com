I&rsquo;ve been meaning to start writing for a while. Most of what I think about — running [StudyPDF](https://studypdf.com), finishing a Master&rsquo;s degree, the slow accumulation of engineering taste — ends up in DMs, voice notes, and half-finished Notion docs. None of it survives.

This is the place where I try to make some of it survive.

## What I plan to write about

Roughly three things, in no particular order.

**Building a company alone.** StudyPDF has 85,000 users and one engineer. I think there&rsquo;s value in honest writing about what that actually looks like — the parts that aren&rsquo;t in the founder folklore. The pricing experiments that didn&rsquo;t work. The week the database died. What it&rsquo;s like to ship a feature on Sunday and answer support emails on Monday.

**Engineering, narrowly.** Notes on specific decisions: why I picked a particular database, what I learned from a migration that went sideways, the small infrastructure choices that compound. I&rsquo;m not writing tutorials. I&rsquo;m writing for the version of me that&rsquo;s about to make the same decision in eighteen months and won&rsquo;t remember why I chose what I chose.

**The dual life.** I&rsquo;m a 22-year-old Master&rsquo;s student at RWTH Aachen running a company that pays my rent and then some. That arrangement is weirder than it sounds, and I don&rsquo;t have many peers to compare notes with. Writing it down is partly for me and partly in case it&rsquo;s useful to someone in a similar spot.

## How often

Not often. The internet doesn&rsquo;t need more low-stakes posting. I&rsquo;d rather publish six things a year that I&rsquo;m happy to point at than fifty that I&rsquo;m not.

If you want to follow along, the [RSS feed](/blog) is probably the move.
